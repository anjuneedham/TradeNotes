## 3. Comment strategy that avoids spam flags
TikTok does not publish its limits and "shadow ban" is not an official status; these are cautious rules of thumb.
**Do:** specific 1–2 sentence comments that respond to the video; real questions; 15–25 genuine comments a day spread across the day; reply to every comment on your own videos in the first hour; warm up a day or two before heavy outreach.
**Don't:** paste the same comment, put links or "DM me" in comments, mass follow/unfollow, use bots or schedulers, buy engagement, mention profits or signals, or continue after a warning (stop for the day, ease off 24–48 h).
If reach drops: compare to your normal range and check account status; if a video is removed, read the reason and fix the pattern instead of reposting.

## 5. Voice-over coaching
Write for the ear: one idea, sentences under ~12 words, hook in 1.5 s, numbers spoken plainly. Five fixes: (1) let your pitch fall at the end of statements; (2) pause before the key number; (3) slow down ~10% (about 140–160 words a minute); (4) record standing and smiling; (5) record sentence by sentence, 2–3 takes each.
Setup: closet or under a duvet; mic ~15 cm away, slightly to the side; test wired earbuds vs the tablet mic on the same sentence.
7-day drill (15 min/day): read one script 5× and record the 5th · re-record slower with a pause before the key number · 10 different hooks, keep 2 · full 30 s standing and smiling · shadow a creator's pace for 2 minutes (not their words) · two real videos, max 3 takes each · review the week and pick one fix.
Self-review: filler words, rising endings, too fast, breath noise, distance changes, echo.
Example (hypothetical numbers): "A seventy percent win rate can still lose you money. *(pause)* Say you win twenty dollars and lose sixty. Seven wins is one forty. Three losses is one eighty. *(pause)* You're down forty dollars over ten trades. *(pause)* Win rate lied to you. Expectancy doesn't. TradeNotes shows it. Free, link in bio." On screen: "Hypothetical example · Not financial advice".

## 6. Quality video from a tablet, no paid software
Free toolkit: built-in screen recorder (iPad: Control Center → Screen Recording; Android: quick-settings recorder); CapCut free tier or TikTok/YouTube Shorts editors (verify an export has no watermark or paywall); CapCut voiceover or the tablet's voice recorder; auto-captions, then fix every word by hand.
Look (from `CONTENT_ENGINE.md`): 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps; background `#0A0F1A`, text `#F5F7FA`, one amber accent `#F2B01E`; captions ~58 px, ≤24 characters per line, 2 lines, key word in amber; quick cuts, no intro, end on a 2 s text card; burned-in "SAMPLE DATA · NOT REAL TRADES" on demos.
Per video (~20–25 min once templated): open `https://tradenotes.dev/demo` (real dashboard, fictional data) · screen-record one idea, 20–45 s · record the voice-over separately · assemble in CapCut, cut silences, captions, label, end card · export 1080p/30 fps · post to TikTok, reuse on Shorts · save the project as a template.
Weekly batch (~5 h): Sunday 90 min record 10 demos + voice-overs; Mon–Fri 20 min/day edit and post; Friday 20 min log views, clicks and signups per video.
