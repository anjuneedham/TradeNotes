## 1. Finding high-intent people for 20–25 TikTok DMs a day
**High intent = they are already feeling the problem or already shopping for a solution.** Score every prospect, then spend the day's DMs from the top.

| Tier | Signal | Daily share (of 25) |
|---|---|---|
| A — hot | Asks for a journal/tool ("what journal do you use?"); posts a spreadsheet or notebook journal; comments on journaling-tool videos asking which is best; complains about a repeating mistake ("moved my stop again"); **comments on or asks a question under YOUR videos** | 15 |
| B — warm | Posts blown account, failed prop-firm challenge, revenge-trading rant, "day N of trying to be disciplined" | 8 |
| C — test | Follows you / likes your videos, no stated pain | 2 |

**Where to look (20 min before sending):**
- Search TikTok for: `trading journal`, `journal my trades`, `tradezella`, `tradervue`, `edgewonk`, `prop firm challenge failed`, `blew my account`, `revenge trading`, `overtrading`, `trading psychology`.
- Open the **comments** on the top 10 videos for those searches. People who write "where do I get this?" or "I need one of these" are your Tier A list.
- Your own video commenters first. Reply to every comment in the first hour; DM anyone who asked a question.
- Skip: signal sellers, "DM me for profits" accounts, accounts under ~2 weeks old, anyone promoting a competing journal (not your user).

**Sequence per person (about 3 min each):**
1. Like 1–2 of their videos and leave one real comment (not a pitch). On TikTok, DM access often depends on follows or mutual follows: don't fight the limits, and stop for the day if the app warns you.
2. DM from `SALES_PLAYBOOK.md`, referencing something specific. **No link in message 1.** End on a question: "What do you use to track trades right now?"
3. After they reply, send `{SITE}/?src=tt-dm` and offer a 10-minute setup call.
4. After they have logged trades, offer the December cohort price (decision pending in `HQ.md`).
5. One follow-up on day 3, then stop. Never send the same text twice.

**Track in a sheet** (columns): handle · tier · what they said/posted · date · replied? · interested? · signed up? · logged a trade? · objection.
**Review after every 100 DMs:** reply rate by tier. Drop the worst tier, copy the wording from the best-replying messages. Change one thing at a time.
**Realistic expectation (assumption):** 20–40% of Tier A reply, 5–15% of Tier C. If Tier A is below 15% after 50 DMs, the opener is the problem, not the audience.

## 2. Voice-over coaching
**Write for the ear:** one idea per video, sentences under about 12 words, hook inside 1.5 seconds, say numbers plainly ("seventy percent win rate"), no jargon without a 5-word explanation. Read it aloud before recording; if you stumble, rewrite the sentence.

**Voice technique (the five fixes that matter most):**
1. **Downward ending.** End statements with your pitch falling. Rising endings sound unsure.
2. **Pause before the key number.** Half a second of silence makes the number land.
3. **Speed.** Aim for roughly 140–160 words per minute. Most beginners go too fast. Slow down 10%.
4. **Energy.** Record standing up and smiling. It audibly changes the tone.
5. **One take per sentence, not per video.** Record sentence by sentence, 2–3 takes each, keep the best. Cutting the gaps is easy in the editor.

**Free recording setup:** record in a closet or under a duvet (soft surfaces kill echo). Mic about 15 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side to avoid breath pops. Wired earbuds with a built-in mic often beat the tablet's own mic: test both with the same sentence and listen back.

**7-day drill (15 min a day):**
- Day 1: read one script 5 times; record the 5th; listen back for fast speed, rising endings and filler words.
- Day 2: record the same script slower, with a pause before the key number.
- Day 3: hook only: write and record 10 different first sentences; pick the 2 you'd stop scrolling for.
- Day 4: record a full 30-second video standing up, smiling.
- Day 5: shadow one creator whose voice you like, 2 minutes of their delivery, matching pace and pauses (don't copy their words).
- Day 6: record two real videos end to end, no re-doing beyond 3 takes each.
- Day 7: listen to the week's recordings in order and note what improved and the one thing to fix next week.
**Self-review checklist:** filler words (um, so, basically) · endings rise? · too fast? · breath noise · distance changes · room echo.

**Example (F1 "The number lies", rewritten for the ear, hypothetical numbers):**
> "A seventy percent win rate can still lose you money. *(pause)* Say you win twenty dollars, and lose sixty. *(pause)* Seven wins is one forty. Three losses is one eighty. *(pause)* You're down forty dollars for ten trades. *(pause)* Win rate lied to you. *(beat)* Expectancy doesn't. TradeNotes shows it. Free, link in bio."
On screen: "Hypothetical example" and "Not financial advice".
Check the math before posting: 7 × $20 = $140, 3 × $60 = $180, net −$40.

## 3. Quality videos from a tablet with no paid software
**Free toolkit:**
- **Screen recording:** built in (iPad: Control Center → Screen Recording; Android: the built-in screen recorder, usually in quick settings). Turn on Do Not Disturb first.
- **Editing:** CapCut (free tier), or TikTok's own editor and YouTube Shorts' editor. CapCut has paid effects; stick to the free ones and verify an export has no watermark or paywall before relying on it.
- **Voice:** record straight into CapCut's voiceover, or the tablet's voice recorder then import.
- **Captions:** use auto-captions, then fix every wrong word by hand.

**Look (Andrew Builds, from `CONTENT_ENGINE.md`):** 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps; background `#0A0F1A`, text `#F5F7FA`, one amber accent `#F2B01E`; captions about 58 px, max 24 characters per line, 2 lines, key word in amber; quick cuts, no intro, end on a 2-second text card; burned-in label "SAMPLE DATA · NOT REAL TRADES" on demos.

**Workflow per video (about 20–25 min once you have a template):**
1. Open the TradeNotes live site on the tablet and use sample data.
2. Screen-record the demo, one idea only, 20–45 seconds.
3. Record the voice-over separately (section 2).
4. In CapCut: drop in the screen recording, add the voice-over, cut silences, add captions, add the "SAMPLE DATA" label, end text card.
5. Export 1080p, 30 fps. Post to TikTok; reuse the file on Shorts with a new title.
6. Save the finished project as a **template** and duplicate it for each new video, so branding and captions are pre-set.

**Weekly batch (about 5 h):** Sunday 90 min = record 10 screen demos and voice-overs; Mon–Fri 20 min/day = edit and post one or two; Friday 20 min = log views, link clicks and signups per video.

## 4. Comment strategy that avoids spam flags
TikTok does not publish exact limits, and "shadow ban" is not an official status: what people describe is reduced distribution after spam-like behaviour. Everything here is a cautious rule of thumb, not a verified limit.
**Do**
- Comment like a person: 1–2 sentences that respond to something specific in the video (a number, a mistake, a question). Vary the wording every time.
- Add value or ask a real question ("Did you tag that loss as a mistake or a normal loss?"). Questions earn replies, replies build the relationship, and the DM comes after.
- Spread activity through the day, not in one burst. Aim for about 15–25 genuine comments a day, mixed with watching, liking and saving.
- Comment under your own videos and reply to every comment, especially in the first hour.
- Warm up a day or two before heavier outreach: watch, like and comment normally.
**Don't**
- Paste the same comment, a link, or "check my page / DM me" anywhere. No links in comments or in a bio-spam style.
- Mass-follow/unfollow, use bots, schedulers or growth tools, or buy followers or engagement.
- Mention profits, signals or "guaranteed" anything.
- Keep going after a warning, a "too many actions" message or a sudden reach drop. Stop for the day, and ease off for 24–48 hours.
**Check for trouble:** compare a video's views to your normal range, and look at Creator Tools / account status for violations. If a video is removed, read the reason and fix the pattern; don't repost it unchanged.

## 5. TikTok LIVE strategy
**First check you're eligible.** LIVE access has needed a minimum follower count (commonly cited as 1,000) and minimum age; verify in the app under Create → LIVE. If you're not eligible yet, skip to "Until then".
**Format (once eligible): weekly "Journal review live", 30–40 min, same time each week.**
- Open with the promise in one line: "We review one trading journal and find the mistake." Repeat it every few minutes for new arrivals.
- Review SAMPLE or your own data on screen. Ask viewers to type their biggest repeated mistake, then read and respond to as many as you can.
- Teach one idea (expectancy, mistake tagging). End with the free link in your bio and the Journal Challenge invite.
- Pin a comment with what's free, and that this is education, not financial advice.
**Rules**
- No profit claims, no signals, no trade calls, no "copy my trades", and no screen shares of live position P&L presented as proof.
- Moderate: add a moderator or use the comment filters for spam and scam links, and don't engage with "DM me for profits" accounts.
- Announce it in 2–3 videos beforehand ("Live Saturday 6pm, bring your worst trade").
**Measure:** peak viewers, average watch time, comments, link clicks, signups tagged `?src=tt-live`. Judge a live by signups, not viewer count.
**Until then (not eligible):** reply to comments with video replies, run the Challenge in your group, and join other people's lives as a genuine viewer who comments, not a promoter.
**Realistic expectation:** early lives often have single-digit viewers. Treat the first four as practice for the voice and format.
