# Business Plan
## Pricing decision
Launch at **Free / Starter $12/mo ($108/yr) / Pro $24/mo ($216/yr)** (annual = 25% off), not $9/$19:
- Stripe's ~2.9% + $0.30 takes ~6% of a $9 charge vs ~5.4% of $12; the extra $3 barely affects conversion at this price point.
- Competitors (approximate public list prices, **re-verify before quoting**: TradeZella ~$29–49/mo, Tradervue ~$30–50/mo, TraderSync ~$30–80/mo, Edgewonk ~$170/yr, TradesViz has a free tier with paid ~$20–30/mo) leave clear room to be the affordable, beginner-friendly option.
- Pro must cover market-data cost; at $24 a $50–200/mo data licence is covered by 3–10 Pro users.

Not lifetime. Implement as a Stripe coupon `FOUNDING50`, max_redemptions 10, duration repeating 12 months.

## Differentiation
Beginner-first explanations on every metric; honest small-sample and exclusion warnings; mistake-cost tracking; affordable backtesting; founder is a real trader making education content.
