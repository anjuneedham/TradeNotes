## 2. What we collect
- **Account:** your email address and a securely hashed password (we cannot read your password).
- **What you enter:** trades, notes, tags, strategies, account names and balances, and your onboarding answers (experience, market, style, goals).
- **Sign-in records:** when you log in we store your IP address and browser details with the session, for security.
- **Where you came from:** if you arrive through a link tagged with a source (for example from a social post), we remember that tag so we can learn which channels work.
- **Payments:** handled by HandyPay. We never see or store your card number. We receive and keep a record of each payment (amount, currency, date, status and receipt link), the plan, and HandyPay’s customer and subscription identifiers, so we can switch paid features on and off and show your payment history.
- **Emails:** messages we send you (confirmation, password reset) and your replies to support.
## 3. Why we use it
- To provide the service: your journal, statistics and account.
- To keep it secure and prevent abuse.
- To manage subscriptions and respond to support requests.
- To understand which channels bring users and improve the product.
- To meet legal obligations.
We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.
## 4. Who we share it with
Only service providers that help us run TradeNotes, under their own security and privacy terms:
- **Supabase** — database hosting (United States).
- **Vercel** — website hosting and server logs (United States).
- **Resend** — sending our account emails.
- **HandyPay** — payments.
Your data is therefore processed outside Jamaica, mainly in the United States. We may also disclose information where the law requires.
## 5. Cookies
- better-auth.session_token — keeps you logged in (essential; expires after about 30 days).
- tn_src — remembers the source tag of the link you came from (30 days).
- tn_theme — remembers a light or dark appearance if you choose one in Settings (1 year; not set unless you choose).
We do not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies.
## 6. How long we keep it
While your account is open. You can delete your account yourself at any time in Settings → Your data; this immediately removes your profile, trades, notes, subscription record and payment history from our database. If you ask us by email instead, we will do it within 30 [deletionDays — confirm] days. Copies in provider backups expire on the provider’s normal schedule. HandyPay keeps its own payment records as the law requires.
## 7. Your rights
You can download your trades yourself (Settings → Your data → Download my trades), correct any trade by editing it, and delete your account yourself. You can also ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a purpose. Email [supportEmail] from the address on your account; we may ask you to verify your identity. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the data-protection authority where you live — in Jamaica, the Office of the Information Commissioner.
## 8. Security
We use encrypted connections, hashed passwords, rate limiting on sign-in, and database-level rules so each user can only reach their own records. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you we will tell you and the authorities where the law requires.
## 9. Children
TradeNotes is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
## 10. Changes
We will post updates here with a new effective date and tell you about material changes by email.
