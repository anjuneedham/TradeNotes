# Subscription & Cancellation Policy
> **DRAFT — not reviewed by a lawyer.** Generated from `src/app/(marketing)/legal/subscription/page.tsx` by `scripts/legal-to-md.py`. Values in [brackets] come from `src/lib/legal.ts`; `[businessName]` and `[supportEmail]` are still unset. See LEGAL_INFORMATION_SOURCES.md.

## 1. Plans and prices
- **Free** — US$0. Up to 50 trades. No card needed.
- **Standard** — US$12 per month. Up to 2,000 trades.
- **Premium** — US$24 per month. Unlimited trades.
What each plan includes today is listed on the pricing page. Features marked “in development” are not part of any plan until they are released. Prices are in US dollars; your bank may add conversion or foreign-transaction fees, and taxes may apply.
## 2. Billing and renewal
Payments are processed by HandyPay. We never see or store your card number. A paid plan starts when HandyPay confirms your first payment and **renews automatically every month** on the same date until you cancel.
## 3. Failed payments
If a renewal fails, HandyPay retries the card and we email you. Your paid features continue until the end of the period you paid for plus 3 days. If payment still has not succeeded, your account moves to the Free plan.
## 4. Cancelling
Cancel at any time from **Plan & billing** in the app, from the link in your HandyPay receipt, or by emailing [supportEmail]. There is no cancellation fee and no questions to answer first. Cancelling stops future charges; you keep paid features until the end of the period you have already paid for. You can resume before that date.
## 5. Changing plan
To move between Standard and Premium, email [supportEmail]; we move your existing subscription so you are never billed twice. To move to Free, cancel.
## 6. What happens to your data on Free
Nothing is deleted when a paid plan ends. You can view, analyse and export every trade. You can add new trades while you hold fewer than 50.
## 7. Refunds
If you are unhappy, email [supportEmail] within **14 [refundDays — confirm] days of your first payment** for a full refund of that payment. Later payments are not refunded, except where the law requires.
## 8. Deleting your account
If you delete your account in Settings while subscribed, we cancel the subscription first so you are not charged again.
## 9. No promise of trading results
A subscription buys software for recording and reviewing your trades. It does not buy advice, signals or any trading result. See the Risk Disclaimer.
