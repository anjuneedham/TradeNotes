# Decision memo: licensed historical data for chart replay
Written 2026-11-14 for the owner. **Nothing was built and no vendor was contacted.** This updates the 2026-11-08 vendor table in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md and the 2026-11-13 memo docs/BACKTEST_REPLAY_LICENSING.md. All pages were read on 2026-11-14 through a page-fetch tool. This is a reading of public pages, **not legal advice and not a contract answer**; only a vendor's written reply settles display rights.

## One-page recommendation
- **Do not buy data now.** The manual backtest log (Premium) already ships. Replay would add a recurring cost of roughly **$300 to $1,000 per month** (range below) before a single customer uses it. That is 13 to 42 Premium subscribers at $24 just to cover the data.
- **If replay demand shows up, ship "bring your own CSV" first** (cost: $0 data, a few days of build, support effort for formats and time zones). The user supplies candles they already have the right to use; we replay only their own upload, stored under row-level security. The terms must say the user is responsible for the right to use what they upload. A new user table and a privacy-page update would be needed (not built).
- **Send the question below to the four vendors now** (free, an email each). The answers decide whether a licensed tier is realistic. Do not commit to anyone until a written answer says "display to logged-in subscribers of a commercial SaaS is allowed on plan X, at price Y, with caching rule Z".
- Candidate order from the pages I read (not a purchase advice): TraderMade Live plan first (its own pricing page states the exact use we need), Massive Currencies Business second (terms grant display to end users of the customer's own product, but at $999), Twelve Data Venture third (external display listed, forex coverage and history not shown), Tiingo last (forex terms say internal use; display is a separate sales conversation).

## Bring-your-own-CSV option
- What it is: the user uploads candle files (time, open, high, low, close, optional volume) exported from their own broker, MT5 or TradingView. Replay is a chart that reveals one bar at a time from that file. We store only that user's upload.
- Why it is safe on licensing: we do not supply or redistribute data. Residual risk: a user may upload data whose licence forbids it; the terms put that on the user. A TradingView or broker export may itself restrict re-use; the user is responsible. Ask the lawyer to approve the sentence.
- Cost: no data fee; storage is small if capped (for example 20 MB per file, a few files); Supabase Storage allowance matters (the Free tier limit is in the owner checklist). Needs a new table with row-level security, deletion with the account, privacy-page and LEGAL_FILL_IN updates, plan-gate updates. Support cost is the real cost (formats, time zones, gaps, daylight saving).
- Effort (estimate): about 1 to 2 weeks including tests. Not started.

## Question to send each vendor (copy and paste)
> We run a small paid SaaS trading journal. We would like to show historical forex candles (M1 to D1, about 20 major and minor pairs, 5 or more years of history) to our **logged-in paying subscribers** inside our own web app, with a bar-by-bar replay for their backtesting. We do not resell data or expose a feed or API. Please answer in writing: (1) Is this use allowed, and on which plan or licence? (2) What is the monthly or annual price, including any display or non-professional fees? (3) May we **store** the bars in our own database to serve them, and for how long, and what happens to stored data if we cancel? (4) Is a public sample or demo page allowed? (5) What attribution or delay labels are required? (6) Are there per-user, per-request or exchange fees? (7) Is a start-up discount available?
Addresses seen: sales@massive.com (Massive), api@oanda.com (OANDA), the vendors' own contact pages for TraderMade, Twelve Data and Tiingo (Tiingo says "Contact sales"). I did not send anything.
