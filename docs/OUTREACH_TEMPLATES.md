# DM templates: 25 a day (5 partners + 20 traders)

Log every message in `docs/OUTREACH_TRACKER.csv` (or a copy in Google Sheets). Review after 14 days: replies %, signups by `src`/`ref`.
Links: partners get a `?ref=<their-code>` link once they say yes (`node scripts/affiliates.mjs add <code> "<name>" <email>`). Before that, and for traders, use `?src=` tags so signups are traceable:
- `?src=dm-tt` TikTok, `?src=dm-ig` Instagram, `?src=dm-dc` Discord, `?src=dm-x` X, `?src=dm-rd` Reddit.

## Partners (5/day): prop-firm Discords, trading educators, YouTube/TikTok creators with an active audience
**P1: first message (give first, ask later)**
> Hi {name}, I build TradeNotes, a trading journal. I made a free journal template + 30-trade review checklist for prop firm traders: https://tradenotes.dev/free. Happy for you to share it with {group}, no strings. If your members like it, I run a partner program: 30% of what they pay for their first year. Want a free Premium account to try it first?

**P2: day 4 follow-up**
> Quick follow-up: here's the R-multiple + challenge calculator a few traders found useful: https://tradenotes.dev/learn/r-multiple-prop-firm-challenge. Happy to set up a partner link if it's a fit, or just send feedback.

**When they say yes:** create the partner, send their link, the disclosure rule ("say it's an affiliate link; never promise profits"), and the free kit (template, checklist, calculator links).

## Traders (20/day): people posting trades, P&L screenshots, challenge attempts
Don't pitch first. Answer their post, then offer something useful.

**T1: someone who just failed or is starting a challenge**
> Tough one. Something that helped me: work out the challenge in R before trading it. At 1% risk, a 5% daily limit is only 5 losses. I made a free calculator for this: {link}?src=dm-{platform}

**T2: someone posting their trades or asking how to improve**
> Do you track your R per trade? It's the quickest way to see which setups actually pay. Free template here if useful: https://tradenotes.dev/free?src=dm-{platform}

**T3: interested / replied**
> If you want it done automatically, TradeNotes is free for 30 trades and works out R, expectancy and your costliest mistake: https://tradenotes.dev/signup?src=dm-{platform}. I'm the founder, so tell me what's missing and I'll build it. First 100 people get the founding price.

Rules: max 1 follow-up. No copy-paste spam in comments. Never promise results.
