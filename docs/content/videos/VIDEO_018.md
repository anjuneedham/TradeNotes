## 5. Voiceover script
Profit factor is your total winnings divided by your total losses. One point five means for every dollar lost,
you made a dollar fifty. Below one means you're losing overall. But with fewer than thirty trades, it can
swing wildly — two lucky trades can make anyone look great. That's why TradeNotes warns you when your sample
is small.
