## 5. Voiceover script
That loss might have been a good trade. Seriously. If you followed your plan, risked what you meant to, and
the market just went the other way — that's a good trade with a bad outcome. A bad trade is one where you
broke your rules, even if it made money. When you review, sort losses into two piles. Followed plan, or
didn't. Only the second pile needs fixing. The first pile is the cost of doing business. TradeNotes asks 'did
you follow your plan?' on every trade, so you can split them in one click.
