## 5. Voiceover script
A screenshot of a winning trade proves one thing: one trade happened. It doesn't show the losses before it,
the size, or the account. A track record is every trade, wins and losses, over months. Before you copy anyone
— including yourself — ask to see the full list. That's what a journal is.
