## 5. Voiceover script
Some of your worst trades happen because nothing was happening. The market's quiet, you've been staring at it
for an hour, and you take something just to feel like you're trading. That's a boredom trade. They're usually
small, unplanned, and they add up. Next time you log a trade, be honest about how you felt. If the answer is
bored, tag it. After a month, look at every trade tagged 'bored' together. If they're losing, you've just
found free money: the trades you don't take.
