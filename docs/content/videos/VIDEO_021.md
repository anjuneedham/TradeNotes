## 5. Voiceover script
A pip is usually the fourth decimal place in a forex price — from one-oh-eight-five-oh to one-oh-eight-five-
one is one pip. For yen pairs it's the second decimal. On one standard lot of EURUSD a pip is about ten
dollars. Know your pip value before you know your stop size.
