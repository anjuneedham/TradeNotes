## 5. Voiceover script
If you only write three things after a trade, make it these. One: why you took it — before you knew the
result. Two: where your stop was, and whether you moved it. Three: one sentence on what you'd do differently.
That's it. Thirty seconds. Most traders skip this because the trade is over and they want to forget it —
especially the losers. But in a month, those three lines will show you the mistake you keep repeating. You
can't fix a pattern you never wrote down.
