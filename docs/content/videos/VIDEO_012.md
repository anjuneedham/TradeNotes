## 5. Voiceover script
'I don't need a stop loss, I just watch the chart.' Here's the problem. Watching the chart means the decision
to exit happens while you're scared and losing — the worst possible moment to decide anything. A stop moves
that decision to before the trade, when you're calm. It also means you can't calculate your risk, so you can't
size properly, and you can't measure R. If you won't use a hard stop yet, at least write down the price where
you're wrong, before you enter. That's still a plan.
