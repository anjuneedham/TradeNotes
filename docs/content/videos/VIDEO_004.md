## 5. Voiceover script
The most expensive trade usually isn't the loss. It's the one you take right after it. You lose, you feel it,
and within ten minutes you're back in — bigger size, worse setup, no plan. That's revenge trading, and almost
every trader has done it. The fix isn't willpower. It's a rule you decide before the loss happens. Mine: after
a full stop-out, I close the chart for fifteen minutes and write down what happened first. And I tag any trade
I take out of frustration as 'revenge'. If you track it, you can see exactly what that feeling costs you.
