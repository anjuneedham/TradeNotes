## 5. Voiceover script
Let's journal a losing trade. Properly. This is sample data, but it's exactly how I do mine. EURUSD, long, one
lot. Entry one-oh-eight-five-oh. My stop was one-oh-eight-two-oh — so I was risking three hundred dollars. It
hit my stop. Now the important part: did I follow my plan? No. I entered before my setup confirmed. So I tag
it: entered early. And I write one line: wait for the candle to close. That's the trade TradeNotes counts as
minus one R. Next week, when I filter by 'entered early', I'll see what that habit actually costs me.
