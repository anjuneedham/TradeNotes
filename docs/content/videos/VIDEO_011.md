## 5. Voiceover script
Five minutes on a Sunday will teach you more than five hours of trading videos. Here's the review. Minute one:
total result and average R for the week. Minute two: your biggest loss — did you follow your plan? Minute
three: which mistake tag showed up most. Minute four: one thing that went well. Minute five: one rule for next
week. Write the rule somewhere you'll see it Monday morning. That's it. The traders who improve aren't the
ones who watch the most content. They're the ones who look at their own data.
