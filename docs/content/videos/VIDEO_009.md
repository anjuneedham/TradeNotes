## 5. Voiceover script
'I'll just move my stop a little.' Here's what that actually does. You planned to risk fifty dollars. The
trade goes against you, you move the stop, and now you're risking a hundred. Then a hundred and fifty. Your
loss is now three times what you planned — and your journal still says you 'had a stop'. Moving a stop further
away isn't managing a trade. It's changing the plan after it stopped going your way. If it happens, tag it.
'Moved stop' is the most common tag in a lot of journals — and usually the most expensive.
