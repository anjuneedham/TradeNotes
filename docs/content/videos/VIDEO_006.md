## 5. Voiceover script
Most beginners think they need a better strategy. So they switch every two weeks. New indicator, new YouTube
video, new system. Here's the problem: you never find out if the old one worked, because you never followed it
long enough — or never wrote down when you broke the rules. A strategy you follow half the time isn't a
strategy. Before you switch, journal twenty trades with one setup and tag every time you broke a rule. Then
you'll know whether it's the strategy… or you.
