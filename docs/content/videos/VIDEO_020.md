## 5. Voiceover script
If your trading plan doesn't fit on one index card, you won't follow it in the moment. Write: what you trade,
when, your setup in one sentence, where the stop goes, risk per trade, and when you stop for the day. Keep it
next to your screen. Log the strategy name on every trade so you can see if the card works.
