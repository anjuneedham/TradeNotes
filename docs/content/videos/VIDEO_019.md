## 5. Voiceover script
Take your single biggest loss this month and give it ten minutes. What was the plan? Was the size normal? Did
you move the stop? Was it in your best session or your worst? Most big losses aren't bad luck — they're a
normal mistake on a bigger size. Find which one.
