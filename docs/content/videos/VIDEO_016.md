## 5. Voiceover script
Risking one percent per trade sounds boring. That's the point. At one percent, ten losses in a row costs about
ten percent of your account — painful, survivable. At ten percent per trade, the same streak can wipe you out.
Losing streaks happen to every strategy. Your risk per trade decides whether you're still around when the good
trades come. Write your risk in dollars on every trade before you enter.
