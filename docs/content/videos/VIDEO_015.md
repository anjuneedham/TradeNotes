## 5. Voiceover script
Most traders start a journal. Almost none keep it. Usually it's because the journal asks for too much —
screenshots, twenty fields, essays — so after a bad day they skip it, then skip it again. Make it smaller. The
minimum: instrument, size, entry, stop, exit, one mistake tag. Under a minute. Add notes when you have energy.
A short journal you keep for three months beats a perfect one you abandon in a week. TradeNotes only requires
four fields; everything else is optional.
