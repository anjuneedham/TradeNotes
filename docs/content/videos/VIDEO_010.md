## 5. Voiceover script
You might be profitable in one session and giving it all back in another. Most traders never check. Group your
trades by session — Asia, London, New York — and look at the net result and average R for each. A lot of
people find one session that quietly drains their account, often the one where they're tired or bored. You
don't need a new strategy. You might just need to stop trading at three in the afternoon. In TradeNotes, the
session breakdown is on the dashboard — just tag the session when you log the trade.
