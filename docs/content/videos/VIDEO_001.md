## 5. Voiceover script
You win seven out of ten trades… and your account is still going down. Here's why. Your seven wins made thirty
dollars each. Two hundred and ten dollars. Your three losses? A hundred dollars each. Three hundred. Win rate
doesn't pay you. The size of your wins versus your losses does. That's why every trade in my journal gets an R
number — how much I made compared to what I risked. Plus 1R, plus 0.3R, minus 1R. When the average R is
negative, a high win rate just means you're losing slowly. Log your last ten trades and look at the average R,
not the win rate.
