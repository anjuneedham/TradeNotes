## 5. Voiceover script
Traders keep saying 'R'. Here's what it means. R is how much you planned to lose if your stop gets hit. Say
your entry is here, your stop is here, and that distance on your position size is fifty dollars. That's 1R. If
you make a hundred dollars, that's plus 2R. If you lose fifty, minus 1R. Why bother? Because R lets you
compare trades of different sizes. A hundred dollars on a big position might be a worse trade than forty
dollars on a small one. Put a stop on every trade and R does the maths for you.
