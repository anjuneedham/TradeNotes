# Content strategy
## Goal
Make beginner and intermediate traders **recognise a habit in their own trading** and see that writing it down and reviewing it is how you fix it. TradeNotes appears as the obvious tool, not the subject, in most videos.

## Audience
Primary: beginners (0–12 months), mostly forex, mobile-first, learning from TikTok/Reels/Shorts. Secondary: intermediate traders who already lose money to repeatable mistakes.

## Principles
1. **One idea per video**, hook in 2 seconds, 25–45 seconds.
2. **Specific beats motivational.** Numbers, examples, one rule to try. No "grind", no lifestyle, no lambos.
3. **Honesty is the brand.** No profit claims, no fake results/testimonials/user counts. App footage is labelled SAMPLE DATA. Every video: "Not financial advice."
4. **Show only what exists.** Demonstrations use built features (journal, edit, R, tags, analytics, export). Backtesting is not shown as a feature.
5. **Mix:** ~60% education/relatable problems, ~25% habits/psychology, ~15% product demos. Never two demos in a row.

## Pillars → job
| Pillar | Job |
|---|---|
| Trading mistakes, misconceptions | Reach (relatable, shareable) |
| Psychology, discipline, losing trades | Trust (honest, non-hype) |
| Risk management, beginner education, analysis | Saves (useful reference) |
| Journaling, habits | Bridge to the product |
| TradeNotes demos | Conversion (link in bio) |

## Platforms
Same 9:16 master for all three. TikTok: conversational caption + 3–5 tags. Reels: save/share prompt in caption. Shorts: searchable title. Post natively (no watermarks from other platforms).

## Visual identity
Use the existing channel system in `docs/CONTENT_ENGINE.md` (navy `#0A0F1A`, amber accent `#F2B01E`, Inter, burned-in captions ≤24 chars/line). App footage keeps TradeNotes' own UI.

## Measurement (weekly)
Per video: 3-second hold rate, average watch %, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks. Per week: sign-ups with `?src=tt|ig|yt` tags (stored on the profile) → activated (≥5 trades) → paid. Double down on pillars with the best **saves + sign-ups**, not views.

## Link tracking
Bio link: `https://tradenotes.dev/?src=tt-organic` (TikTok), `ig-organic`, `yt-organic`. The first tag a visitor arrives with is stored at sign-up (built and tested).
