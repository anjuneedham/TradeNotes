## The 15 comments
**1. "Is this financial advice?"**
> No. I'm sharing how to journal and review your own trades, not telling anyone what to trade. TradeNotes is a journal. It doesn't give trading advice. Not financial advice.

**2. "Does it make you profitable?"**
> No, and nothing can promise that. TradeNotes records your trades and shows your numbers and habits, like your average R or which mistakes you tag most, so you can see what's happening. What you do with it is up to you.

**3. "How much is it?"**
> There's a free plan (up to 50 trades, no card). Paid plans are Standard $12/month and Premium $24/month, or $120 / $240 a year, in USD. Full details are on the pricing page: tradenotes.dev/pricing.

**4. "Is it free?"**
> The Free plan is free with no card needed: up to 50 trades with the journal, R-multiples, tags, analytics and CSV export. Paid plans add things like a higher trade limit, broker import, the calendar, reviews and the playbook.

**5. "What's the catch with the free plan?"**
> The limit is 50 trades and 1 trading account. Nothing else is hidden: the feature list per plan is on the pricing page.

**6. "Is there a free trial?" / "Can I try the paid plans?"** *(hold until the owner confirms trial wording)*
> The Free plan is the best way to try it: no card, and it has the core journal and analytics. For how paid plans are billed, the pricing page and tradenotes.dev/legal/subscription have the current details.

**7. "Refunds?" / "Can I get my money back?"**
> Yes. Email support@tradenotes.dev within 14 days of your first payment and you get a full refund of that payment. Payments after the first aren't refunded, except where the law requires. You can also cancel any time and keep access to the end of the period you paid for.

**8. "Can I cancel any time?"**
> Yes: in Plan & billing in the app, from the link in your Handypay receipt, or by emailing support@tradenotes.dev. No cancellation fee. You keep paid features until the end of the period you've paid for, then your account goes back to Free and nothing is deleted.

**9. "Does it connect to my broker / auto-import?"**
> Not live. There's no broker auto-sync. On Standard and Premium you can import a CSV or broker export (it recognises several formats, including MetaTrader 4/5 and cTrader); on the Free plan you add trades by hand.

**10. "Does it do backtesting?"**
> Not yet. Backtesting and strategy comparison are listed as in development on the pricing page, with no date promised.

**11. "Is it for forex / futures / crypto / stocks?"**
> The journal handles forex (lots and pips), futures, options and crypto specs. Check the app with a few trades on the Free plan to see if it fits your instrument.

**12. "Is my data safe? Who sees my trades?"**
> For exactly how your data is stored and used, the Privacy Policy is at tradenotes.dev/legal/privacy. You can export your trades as CSV and delete your account in Settings.
*Owner: I did not read the full privacy page; confirm the export and account-deletion claims (Settings) before use.*

**13. "Which plan do I need?"**
> Start free. If you hit the 50-trade limit or want the calendar, reviews, playbook or broker import, that's Standard. Premium is for unlimited trades and up to 10 accounts. Plan comparison: tradenotes.dev/pricing.

**14. "Is this a scam?" / "Another app trying to sell me a course?"**
> Fair question. It's a trading journal with a free plan, not a course or a signal service, and it doesn't promise profits. The company details and terms are on the site's legal pages.

**15. "Can you review my trades / tell me what to trade?"**
> I can't give trading advice or review individual trades for you. What I can share is how to look at your own journal: average R, mistakes you tag, which session you trade worst. Not financial advice.

## Other likely replies (short)

- **Someone says "I made $X using this":** thank them but never repost it as a result or testimonial without **written permission** (`SALES_PLAYBOOK.md`).
- **Support issue (can't log in, billing):** "Email support@tradenotes.dev and we'll sort it."
