# VO_011: The 5-minute weekly review
Written by: Claude Opus 5.5

## Narration
Five minutes on Sunday can teach you more than five hours of trading videos. Here's the review. Minute one. Your total result and average R for the week. Minute two. Your biggest loss. Did you follow your plan? Minute three. Which mistake tag showed up most? Minute four. One thing that went well. Minute five. One rule for next week. Write that rule where you'll see it on Monday morning. More videos can't show you your own habits. Your data can. Save this, and do it this Sunday.

## Delivery notes
- Warm, unhurried hook. Stress "five minutes" and "five hours".
- Say each "Minute one", "Minute two" as a label, with a half-second pause after it, so it lines up with the tile flips.
- Stress "one" in "One rule for next week". It is the most important step.
- Pause before "More videos can't show you your own habits." Then a short beat and a slightly lower "Your data can."
- Friendly CTA, like a suggestion, not an order.
