# VO_001: Your win rate is lying to you
Written by: Claude Opus 5.5

## Narration
You win seven out of ten trades. A seventy percent win rate. Your account is still going down. Here's why. Each win made thirty dollars. Seven wins, two hundred and ten. Each loss cost a hundred. Three losses, minus three hundred. Net, minus ninety dollars. Win rate doesn't pay you. The size of your wins and losses does. That's why every trade in my journal gets an R number. Your result divided by what you risked. Each win was plus zero point three R. Each loss, minus one R. Average R, minus zero point zero nine R. Check your average R. Not your win rate.

## Delivery notes
- Calm and matter of fact, like pointing out something on a friend's screen. No hype, no "gotcha" tone.
- Land the hook fast: "seven out of ten trades" inside the first two seconds, then a short beat before "Your account is still going down."
- Read the money lines evenly, one number per phrase. Pause for a full beat after "Net, minus ninety dollars." This is the turn of the video.
- Stress "doesn't pay you" and "size". In the last line, stress "average" and "not".
- Say "R" as the letter "ar". "Minus zero point zero nine R" is the hardest phrase: slow down and keep each word clear.
- End level and quiet. No rising inflection on "Not your win rate."
