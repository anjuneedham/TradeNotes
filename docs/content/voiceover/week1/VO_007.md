# VO_007: Not all losses are mistakes
Written by: Claude Opus 5.5

## Narration
That loss might have been a good trade. You followed your plan and risked what you meant to. The market just went the other way. That's a good trade with a bad outcome. A bad trade breaks your rules, even if it makes money. When you review, sort losses into two piles. Followed the plan, or broke it. Only the second pile needs fixing. The first pile is part of trading. TradeNotes asks, did you follow your plan, on every trade. Share this with a trader who beats themselves up over losses.

## Delivery notes
- Reassuring and thoughtful. The viewer is probably hard on themselves; sound like you are taking weight off them.
- Hook in the first two seconds: stress "good" in "might have been a good trade". Leave a beat after it.
- Contrast is the core: stress "good trade" and "bad outcome", then "breaks your rules" and "makes money".
- Say "Followed the plan, or broke it." as two clear options with a pause at the comma.
- Stress "second" in "Only the second pile needs fixing." Say "The first pile is part of trading." calmly, almost as an aside.
- Read the app line as the question it is: "did you follow your plan" with a light question tone. Gentle on the CTA.
