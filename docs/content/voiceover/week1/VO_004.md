# VO_004: The trade after the loss
Written by: Claude Opus 5.5

## Narration
The most expensive trade usually isn't the loss. It's the one you take right after it. You lose. You feel it. Ten minutes later, you're back in. Bigger size. Worse setup. No plan. That's revenge trading. The fix isn't willpower. It's a rule you set before the loss happens. Here's mine. After a full stop-out, I close the chart for fifteen minutes and write down what happened first. And any trade I take out of frustration gets tagged revenge trade. Tag it, and you can see what that feeling costs you. Follow for more honest trading habits.

## Delivery notes
- Calm, honest and a little personal. This is about a feeling most viewers know, so sound understanding, never preachy.
- Hook in the first two seconds: stress "most expensive" and "isn't". Short pause, then "It's the one you take right after it."
- Make "You lose. You feel it." slow and quiet, then pick up slightly for "Ten minutes later, you're back in."
- Read "Bigger size. Worse setup. No plan." as three clipped beats, each a little firmer than the last.
- Stress "before" in "a rule you set before the loss happens". Pause after "Here's mine."
- Warm and level on the CTA. No push.
