# VO_002: The 3 things to write after every trade
Written by: Claude Opus 5.5

## Narration
If you only write three things after a trade, write these. One. Why you took it, before you knew the result. Two. Where your stop was, and if you moved it. Three. One sentence on what you'd change. That's it. Thirty seconds. Most traders skip this after a loss. They just want to forget it. But a month of these notes can show you the mistake you keep repeating. You can't fix what you never wrote down. Save this for your next trade.

## Delivery notes
- Friendly and practical, like giving a tip to someone sitting next to you. No lecturing.
- Hook straight away: "three things" should land within the first two seconds. Stress "three".
- Say "One." "Two." "Three." as their own short beats, with a half-second pause after each number so the card flips land on them.
- Stress "before" in "before you knew the result" and "moved" in "if you moved it".
- Soften slightly on "They just want to forget it." It is relatable, not a criticism.
- Slow down for "You can't fix what you never wrote down." Pause before "Save this for your next trade."
