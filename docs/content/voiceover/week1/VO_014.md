# VO_014: Boredom trades
Written by: Claude Opus 5.5

## Narration
Some of your worst trades happen because nothing was happening. The market's quiet, and you've stared at it for an hour. So you take something just to feel busy. That's a boredom trade. They're small, unplanned, and they add up. When you log a trade, be honest about how you felt. If the answer is bored, tag it. After a month, look at your bored trades together. If they're losing, the fix costs nothing. Don't take them. Comment bored if this is you.

## Delivery notes
- Start quietly, almost flat, to match the sparse ticking music. Stress "nothing" in the hook.
- Slow and a little drawn out on "stared at it for an hour", so the boredom is felt.
- Light stress on "That's a boredom trade." as the name of the thing. Then pick up slightly for "small, unplanned, and they add up", one beat per item.
- Stress "honest" and "bored". Gentle, never preachy.
- Pause before "Don't take them." Say it simply and clearly. Smile on the CTA.
