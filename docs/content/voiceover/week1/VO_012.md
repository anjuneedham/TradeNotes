# VO_012: 'I don't use a stop, I watch the chart'
Written by: Claude Opus 5.5

## Narration
"I don't need a stop loss. I just watch the chart." Here's the problem. Watching the chart means you decide to exit while you're scared and losing. That's the worst moment to decide anything. A stop moves that decision to before the trade, while you're calm. Without a stop, you can't know your risk, size properly, or measure R. If you won't use a hard stop yet, write down where you're wrong before you enter. That's still a plan. Follow for more beginner mistakes, explained.
