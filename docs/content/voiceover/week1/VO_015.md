## Narration
Most traders start a journal. Almost none keep it.

Usually the journal asks too much. Screenshots, twenty fields, an essay. One bad day, and you skip it.

So make it smaller. Instrument, size, entry, stop, exit, and one mistake tag. That takes under a minute.

A short journal you keep for three months beats a perfect one you quit in a week.

TradeNotes requires only four fields: instrument, size, entry price and time. The rest is optional.

## Delivery notes
- Pace: steady and unhurried, about 2.4 to 2.5 words per second. Do not speed up to fit. The script is already short enough.
- Hook: say "Most traders start a journal" plainly, then a half-beat pause, then "Almost none keep it" slightly lower and slower. Stress "none". No drama.
- Lists: give each item its own small beat. "Screenshots, twenty fields, an essay" should sound a little tired. "Instrument, size, entry, stop, exit" should sound light and easy, like ticking boxes, because the checklist items tick on screen one by one.
- Pauses: a clear pause before "So make it smaller" (this is the turn of the video), and a short pause after "under a minute" before the three months line.
- Stress words: "too much", "smaller", "one" mistake tag, "three months", "four" fields, "optional".
- Tone: a friend who has quit a journal before and is telling you the fix. Encouraging, never preachy, never hype. End on "The rest is optional" with a falling, relaxed tone, then stay silent over the end card.
