# VO_005: What '1R' means (in 30 seconds)
Written by: Claude Opus 5.5

## Narration
Traders keep saying one R. Here's what it means. R is what you planned to lose if your stop hits. Entry here. Stop here. On your size, that gap is fifty dollars. That's one R. Make a hundred, plus two R. Lose fifty, minus one R. R lets you compare trades of different sizes. A hundred dollar win that risked two hundred is plus zero point five R. A forty dollar win that risked twenty is plus two R. Enter a stop, and TradeNotes works out R. Save this for when someone says two R.

## Delivery notes
- Clear teacher tone for absolute beginners. Patient, never condescending.
- Hook in the first two seconds: "Traders keep saying one R." Stress "one R", then a short pause.
- "Entry here. Stop here." are pointing beats. Leave a half-second gap after each so the lines can draw in.
- Say "R" as the letter "ar" every time. Stress "plus" and "minus" so the sign is never missed.
- The comparison is the key idea: slow down on "risked two hundred" and "risked twenty", and stress "zero point five" and "two".
- Light and friendly on the CTA.
