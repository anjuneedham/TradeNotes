# VO_006: 'I just need a better strategy'
Written by: Claude Opus 5.5

## Narration
You don't need a new strategy. You need to know if you followed the last one. Most beginners switch every two weeks. New indicator. New video. New system. So they never find out if the old one worked. If you follow a strategy fifty percent of the time, you haven't tested it. Before you switch, journal twenty trades with one setup. Tag every rule break. Then you'll know if it's the strategy, or you. Comment the strategy you switched away from.

## Delivery notes
- Direct but kind. A friend telling a hard truth, not a guru scolding.
- Hook in the first two seconds: stress "don't" and "new". Short pause, then "You need to know if you followed the last one," stressing "followed".
- Read "New indicator. New video. New system." quickly and evenly, matching the fast card shuffle.
- Slow down on "fifty percent of the time, you haven't tested it." Stress "tested".
- Give "twenty trades" and "one setup" equal weight, like a checklist.
- Pause before "or you." Say it gently, with a slight smile. Relaxed on the CTA.
