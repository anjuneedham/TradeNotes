## Narration
You might be profitable in London and giving it all back in New York. Have you checked? Group your trades by session. Asia, London, New York. Compare net result and average R for each. Sometimes one session quietly drains the account, often the one where you're tired or bored. You might not need a new strategy. You might just need to stop trading at three in the afternoon. In TradeNotes, pick the session when you log a trade. The analytics page breaks it down. Free journal with session breakdowns. Link in bio.

- Say "Asia, London, New York" slowly, as a list, one beat each.
- Stress "quietly". Keep it matter of fact, not dramatic.
- The two "You might" lines are a pair. Stress "new strategy" in the first and "stop trading" in the second, with a small pause between them.
- Product lines plain and practical, like showing a friend where a setting is. No sales voice on the CTA.
