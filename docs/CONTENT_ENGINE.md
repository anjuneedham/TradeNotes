## Starter scripts
### 1. "Your win rate is lying to you"
- **Hook:** "I had a 70% win rate and lost money. Here's why."
- **VO:** "Win rate only tells you how often you're right — not how much. Seven small wins of $50 and three losses of $200 is a 70% win rate… and minus $250. The number that matters is expectancy: your total result divided by your number of trades. If it's negative, a high win rate just means you're losing slowly. Journal your trades and look at average win versus average loss — that's where the truth is."
- **Screen:** TradeNotes /learn → expectancy card; then a calculator overlay of the example.
- **Captions:** "70% win rate ≠ profitable" / "Expectancy = total P&L ÷ trades".
- **CTA:** "Free journal that calculates this for you — link in bio."
- **Title:** Why a high win rate can still lose money · **Edit:** punch-in on numbers, red/green colour on wins/losses.
