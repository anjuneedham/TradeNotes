From 100 to 1,000

An illustrative path, not a forecast. Ten times the members in twelve months needs about 21% growth every month; in six months it needs about 47%. Pick the pace on purpose.

Stage 1: 100

Founder-led. Every signup is known by name.

  • DMs, partners, Journal Challenge
  • Manual support, manual payouts
  • Proves who converts and why

Stage 2: 250 to 500

Repeatable channels.

  • Lifecycle emails run themselves
  • 20+ partners, a content engine that works
  • Billing and payouts mostly automated

Stage 3: 1,000

About $18,000 a month at list price.

  • Needs about 30–50 new members a month just to hold, at 3–5% churn
  • Paid tiers of email, database and monitoring
  • Support beyond one person

What HQ should and should not have

HQ exists to answer one question each morning: what do I do today to get the next paying member.

Should have

  • Goal versus actual: members, trials, revenue, days left
  • The funnel by source: signup, logged a trade, trial, paid
  • A payments panel: failed, trial ending, paid but still on Free
  • People stuck before their first trade, with the step they stopped at
  • DM, partner and content pipelines with next actions
  • Email results and unsubscribes
  • This roadmap, with each bar marked done or late
  • An experiment log: what changed, what moved

Should not have

  • Any view of a member's trades, notes or screenshots
  • Edit or delete access to customer data
  • Card numbers or payment secrets
  • Vanity numbers that lead to no decision
  • Automatic messages to customers sent without review
  • Public access: owner sign-in only, with a read-only database role at first
  • Claims about features that do not exist
