# Sales Playbook
## Who to contact (20–30/day)
Traders posting about losses, discipline, "need to journal", prop-firm challenges; commenters on journaling/backtesting videos. Personalise every message to something they posted.

## Cold DM
> Hey {name} — saw your post about {specific thing}. I've been trading ~7 years and kept making the same mistakes until I started journaling properly, so I'm building TradeNotes, a simple journal that shows things like what your mistakes actually cost you. Would you be up for trying it free and telling me what's confusing? No pressure either way.

## Follow-up (day 3)
> Just bumping this in case it got buried — happy to set your account up with you on a 10-minute call if that's easier.

## Founding-member close
> You've been one of the most useful testers. I'm opening 10 founding spots: 50% off for 12 months in return for a 20-min feedback call each month. Want one?

## Demo flow (10 min)
1. Their pain (2 min) → 2. Log one of their real recent trades live (3) → 3. Import CSV (2) → 4. Dashboard: expectancy + mistake tags (2) → 5. Offer (1).

## Launch announcement (founder)
> After 7 years of trading, the thing that changed my results most wasn't a new strategy — it was writing down every trade and measuring my mistakes. I built TradeNotes to make that easy for newer traders. It's free to start. I'd love your honest feedback.

## Welcome emails
D0: welcome + "log your first trade" · D2: "what is expectancy?" · D5: "tag your mistakes" · D10: weekly review prompt + feedback ask · D14 (free): upgrade offer.

## Feedback request
> What almost stopped you from logging a trade? What's one number you wish TradeNotes showed you?
Never use feedback as a testimonial without written permission.

## Launch-week outreach: 25 TikTok + 25 Instagram (manual only)
- **Manual, personalised, spread through the day.** No automation, no copy-paste blasts, no buying followers. Stop for the day if a platform warns or limits you. I have **not verified** current DM limits: TikTok restricts who you can message (often mutual follows) — comment first or follow, then DM; Instagram may limit new-account DMs and push cold DMs to "requests".
- Target: people posting about losses, journaling, discipline, prop-firm challenges, or asking for a journal/backtesting tool. Personalise to something they actually said.
- **Tag every link** so the test can tell platforms apart: TikTok `{SITE}/?src=tt-dm`, Instagram `{SITE}/?src=ig-dm`. Log each DM in a sheet: handle, platform, date, replied?, interested?, signed up?, objection.
- **Instagram variant:** "Hey {name} — saw your story about {specific}. I'm a forex trader (7 yrs) building a free journal that shows what your mistakes actually cost you. Want to try it and tell me what's confusing? {SITE}/?src=ig-dm" (shorter; no links in the very first message if the account is new — ask first, send the link after they reply).
- Follow-up once at day 3; then stop. Never send the same message twice to one person. No profit claims, no testimonials you don't have.
- Founding offer (50% off 12 months, first 10 payers) only **after** someone has actually logged trades; it is delivered manually until Handypay discounts are confirmed.
