# $50 / 5-day paid test — TikTok vs Instagram
## What the research changes (2026-11-10; secondary + official help-center sources, verify in the ad managers)
- **TikTok Ads Manager minimums: $50/day per campaign and $20/day per ad group** (TikTok help center, updated July 2026). $50 over 5 days = $10/day, **below the floor**. A TikTok paid arm needs roughly ≥$100 for 5 days at the ad-group floor, likely ~$250 at the campaign floor. Sources disagree on lifetime-budget floors; check the live help page. → **A TikTok vs Instagram *paid* split is not possible with $50.**
- **TikTok policy:** forex/trading products are restricted or prohibited in ads; a *journaling tool* is not a financial product, but ads that mention forex results, profit or "signals" risk rejection or account flags. Not verified for software journals specifically.
- **Meta/Instagram policy:** financial products/services have special-category rules (licensing in some countries, e.g. UK FCA). A journaling tool is likely outside it, but **no profit claims**, no "guaranteed" language. Do not use "cloaking" or review-evasion tools (several vendors advertise them) — it gets accounts banned.
- Honest statistics: with $50 and ~25 DMs per platform, you can detect a **big** difference, not a small one.

## Decision (mine)
1. **Spend the whole $50 on Instagram** (Meta Ads Manager). TikTok is judged on **organic** content + DMs only.
2. Decide "which platform is better" on a **combined scorecard** of free + paid signals, not on ad cost alone.

## Instagram ad setup
- Objective: Traffic → landing page `{SITE}/?src=ig-ad`. One campaign, one ad set, **$10/day × 5 days**, placements Instagram Reels + Feed only.
- Audience: US, UK, Canada, Australia, 18+; interests like forex/day trading/trading psychology. Keep broad enough for delivery.
- Creative: 2 vertical videos in the Andrew Builds frame (best organic F1 "number lies" + F2 "log a trade"); no income/profit claims; "Not financial advice" on screen. Text: "A free trading journal that shows what your mistakes cost you."
- Rules: don't edit the ad for the first 48 h; check for disapproval each morning; **pause if you've spent $15 with zero clicks**, and tell me the numbers.
